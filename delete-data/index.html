Delete your Emdalex Streams data

This page explains how to delete the data of Emdalex Streams (iPhone and Android), developed by Serhii Krotkykh.

Emdalex Streams has no servers of its own. The developer never receives or stores your data, so there is no account with us to delete. Your data lives in only two places: your YouTube channel and your phone. You can delete it from both yourself, as described below.

1. Delete your streams and recordings

Streams and their recordings belong to your YouTube channel.

  1. In the app: open the stream on the Scheduled, Ready or Completed tab, tap ⋯ → Delete → confirm. The broadcast, its recording and its description (including the location text, if you added it) are deleted from YouTube.
  2. Without the app: open YouTube Studio → Content → Live, select the streams → More actions → Delete forever.

2. Remove the app's access to your Google Account

  1. In the app, open the side menu and tap Log Out. This removes the sign-in from the phone and revokes the app's access.
  2. If you no longer have the phone, or want to be sure: go to myaccount.google.com/connections (for a group channel — the group's Brand Account, see Support), tap Emdalex Streams → Delete all → Confirm.

3. Delete the data on your phone

Uninstall Emdalex Streams. This deletes everything the app keeps on the phone: the sign-in, your settings, cached thumbnails and the app's anonymous device ID (used only to recognise which streams were created on this phone).

Request deletion or ask a question. If you cannot use the steps above, e-mail sergey.krotkih@gmail.com with the subject “Emdalex Streams data deletion”.
