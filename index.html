Out of space and need to film now? Go live — the video lands on your own YouTube channel, ready to watch or download later. Nothing is stored on your phone.

Emdalex Streams turns your iPhone or Android phone into a live camera for your own YouTube channel. Press Stream and you are live; or schedule a stream and the app starts it at the right moment by itself. Go live from the camera or from videos on your phone — or share a video to the app from Photos and it goes live by itself. When you are done, the recording is already on YouTube — it takes no space on your phone and is there for everyone you share the channel with.

No servers in between: your phone talks only to YouTube. No account with us, no data collected.

Share any video straight to your YouTube In Photos, Gallery or Files select one video or several, tap Share → Emdalex Streams — and they go to your own YouTube channel as one stream, one after another. When it ends, the recording is on YouTube and the videos can leave your phone. Works with videos you filmed, videos sent to you in a chat, screen recordings — whatever is on the phone.
How it works
Quick or scheduledGo live with one button, or set a start time and a duration — the stream starts and finishes on its own.
Camera or video filesStream live from the camera, or pick one or several videos from your library — they play one after another as a live stream, with a short black pause between them. Or simply share them to the app from Photos or Gallery.
PauseNeed a moment? Pause the stream: viewers see “Paused — back soon”, the camera and microphone are off, and Resume goes on in the same stream.
For a family or a groupOne shared YouTube channel for everyone. Your own streams are marked in the list: the app recognizes them by an ID it adds to the stream description on YouTube.
Built-in playerWatch recordings right in the app and move from one stream to the next with the previous and next buttons.
Open from YouTubeFound one of your streams in the YouTube app? Tap Share → Emdalex Streams: the app opens right on that stream — scheduled, live or finished. Not signed in yet? It takes you there right after you sign in. How it works
Never miss a startA reminder when it is time for your stream and a notice when someone in the group goes live — even while you are streaming yourself. All quiet: no sounds or vibration, so nothing leaks into a stream.
Unlisted by defaultStreams are unlisted unless you choose private or public. Edit the title, notes and cover when you finish; delete a stream and it is gone from YouTube too.
No servers in betweenVideo goes from your phone straight to YouTube. We do not collect, store or see any of your data.

Good to know

It is a live stream, so it needs an internet connection — mobile data or Wi-Fi. The recording on YouTube has the quality of the stream (720p, or 1080p if chosen in Settings); on a weak connection the app lowers the quality rather than stop. For a recording in your camera's full quality, use the phone's own camera app.

How it works with Google

The app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf to create, start, stop and delete live streams on your channel and to list your recordings. It stores nothing outside your device and your YouTube account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Availability

Emdalex Streams is available for iPhone on the App Store; the Android version is coming soon to Google Play. It requires a Google account with a YouTube channel that has live streaming enabled.
