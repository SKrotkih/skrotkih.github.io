Emdalex Stream

Live video from your iPhone to your own YouTube channel.

Emdalex Stream turns your iPhone into a live camera for your own YouTube channel. Press Stream and you are live; or schedule a stream and the app starts it at the right moment by itself. When you are done, the recording is already on YouTube — it takes no space on your phone and is there for everyone you share the channel with.

Quick or scheduledGo live with one button, or set a start time and a duration — the stream starts and finishes on its own.
For a family or a groupOne shared YouTube channel; everyone signs in with their own Google account. See who is on air and watch with one tap.
Private by defaultStreams are unlisted or private on your channel. Delete one and it is gone from YouTube too.
No servers in betweenVideo goes from your phone straight to YouTube. We do not collect, store or see any of your data.

How it works with Google

The app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf to create, start, stop and delete live streams on your channel and to list your recordings. It stores nothing outside your device and your YouTube account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Availability

Emdalex Stream is available for iPhone on the App Store. It requires a Google account with a YouTube channel that has live streaming enabled.
