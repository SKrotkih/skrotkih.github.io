Emdalex Streams turns your iPhone or Android phone into a live camera for your own YouTube channel. Press Stream and you are live; or schedule a stream and the app starts it at the right moment by itself. Go live from the camera or from a video on your phone. When you are done, the recording is already on YouTube — it takes no space on your phone and is there for everyone you share the channel with.

Quick or scheduledGo live with one button, or set a start time and a duration — the stream starts and finishes on its own.
Camera or video fileStream live from the camera, or pick a video from your library and broadcast it as a live stream.
For a family or a groupOne shared YouTube channel for everyone. Your own streams are marked in the list: the app recognizes them by an ID it adds to the stream description on YouTube.
Built-in playerWatch recordings right in the app and move from one stream to the next with the previous and next buttons.
Open from YouTubeFound one of your streams in the YouTube app? Tap Share → Emdalex Streams: the app opens right on that stream — scheduled, live or finished. Not signed in yet? It takes you there right after you sign in. How it works
Never miss a startA reminder when it is time for your stream, a notice when someone in the group goes live, and a chime once you are on air.
Unlisted by defaultStreams are unlisted unless you choose private or public. Edit the title, notes and cover when you finish; delete a stream and it is gone from YouTube too.
No servers in betweenVideo goes from your phone straight to YouTube. We do not collect, store or see any of your data.

How it works with Google

The app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf to create, start, stop and delete live streams on your channel and to list your recordings. It stores nothing outside your device and your YouTube account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Availability

Emdalex Streams is available for iPhone on the App Store; the Android version is coming soon to Google Play. It requires a Google account with a YouTube channel that has live streaming enabled.
