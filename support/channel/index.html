Your own YouTube channel

Emdalex Streams streams to a YouTube channel that belongs to you. If you already watch YouTube with a Google account, you are halfway there: you only need to create a channel on that account (once) and let YouTube switch on live streaming for it (once). This page shows both.

Streaming together with your family or a group into one shared channel? See A channel for your group instead — members of a group channel do not need a channel of their own.

  • 2. Create the channel

1. A Google account

Any Gmail address is a Google account. If you don't have one, create it at accounts.google.com/signup — it is free. Use this same account everywhere below and when you sign in to Emdalex Streams.

2. Create the channel

Skip this step if you have uploaded videos or commented on YouTube before — you already have a channel.

The easiest way: from Emdalex Streams

  1. Open Emdalex Streams and sign in with your Google account.
  2. If the account has no channel, the app shows This Google account has no YouTube channel yet. Tap Create a YouTube channel — YouTube opens on the page for creating a channel, already signed in.
  3. Check the name and the handle and tap Create channel.
  4. Return to the app: it notices the channel by itself, no need to sign in again. Then go on with step 3.

You can also create the channel on YouTube yourself:

On the phone, in the YouTube app

  1. Open the YouTube app and make sure you are signed in with your Google account.
  2. Tap You (your picture, bottom right).
  3. If you see Create a channel, tap it. (If you see your name and View channel instead, the channel already exists — go to step 3 below.)
  4. Check the name and the handle YouTube suggests — your own name is fine — and tap Create channel.

Or on a computer

  1. Go to youtube.com and sign in.
  2. Click your picture (top right) → Create a channel.
  3. Confirm the name and the handle and click Create channel.

3. Enable live streaming

Every YouTube channel starts with live streaming switched off. Until it is on, Emdalex Streams cannot create streams for the channel — the app shows “Live streaming is not enabled yet” and brings you here. You do this once per channel; it takes two minutes plus a wait of up to 24 hours.

This step is easiest on a computer. On a phone, open the links below in the browser and, if YouTube offers its app, choose to stay in the browser.
  1. Verify your phone number. Open youtube.com/verify, enter your phone number, then the code from the SMS. This proves the channel belongs to a real person; the number is not shown anywhere.
  2. Ask for live streaming. Open studio.youtube.com and click Create (top right, the camera icon with a plus) → Go live.
  3. YouTube shows a countdown — “Only 23:59 until you can stream”. That's all: live streaming switches on by itself within 24 hours. You can close the page.

If you already see the Live Control Room instead of a countdown, streaming is already on.

The YouTube app may say that going live from a phone needs 50 subscribers. That rule is for the YouTube app's own camera; Emdalex Streams sends video like a streaming program and needs only the steps above.

If it still does not work after 24 hours

  1. Check that you signed in to Emdalex Streams with the same channel: the side menu shows the channel name at the top. If it is a different one, tap Log Out and sign in again — see Sign in.
  2. Open YouTube Studio → Create → Go live once more. If YouTube shows a restriction notice, the notice explains what to do.
  3. YouTube allows live streaming only to channels with no live-streaming restrictions in the past 90 days, and only to account holders aged 18 or over (or 13 and over with a parent's permission, depending on the country).

4. Check in the app

  1. Open Emdalex Streams and sign in with the same Google account. If Google asks to choose a channel, tap your own name.
  2. Tap the round red ▶ button. When the label at the bottom shows LIVE, everything works — tap the stop button to finish.
