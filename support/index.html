Emdalex Streams — Support

Live video from your phone to your own YouTube channel.

Need help? Write to sergey.krotkih@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, iOS or Android version and, if possible, a screenshot — we usually answer within two business days.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and sign in with your Google account. If you manage several YouTube channels, pick the one you want to stream to.
  2. Enter your name when asked — it is added to your streams so the people you share the channel with can tell them apart.
  3. Press Stream to go live right away, or Schedule a new stream to set a start time and a duration.
  4. Scheduled streams start by themselves at their start time while the app is open; if the app is in the background, you get a notification at that moment.
  5. Press Finish to end a stream. The recording appears on the Completed tab a moment later.

Guides

Step-by-step: enabling live streaming on your channel, creating a shared YouTube channel for your group and removing the app's access to your Google Account.

Streaming as a group

Several people can stream to one channel and watch each other. Set up a YouTube Brand Account channel for the group and add its members as Managers (Google Account → Brand Accounts → Manage permissions). Each member signs in to the app with their own Google account and chooses the group channel on the “Choose your account or a brand account” screen.

Please add members on myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts, not through “Permissions” in YouTube Studio — people invited through YouTube Studio cannot use the channel from apps like this one.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my videos?

On your YouTube channel — the app stores nothing on the phone and has no servers of its own. Open YouTube Studio to see, edit or download any recording.

Who can see my streams?

New streams are unlisted: only people with the link (or with access to the channel) can watch them, and they never appear in search or recommendations. You can choose private or public when scheduling a stream.

The Live tab shows someone else’s stream — how do I watch it?

Tap it. The first time, the app asks whether to open streams in its built-in browser or in the YouTube app; you can change this choice later in Settings. Live video on YouTube runs with a delay of about 10–30 seconds — that is normal.

A stream did not start at its scheduled time

The app starts a stream only on the phone of the person who created it, and only while it is open or after that person taps the “Time to go live” notification. Make sure notifications are allowed for Emdalex Streams in the phone’s Settings and that the phone is not in Low Power Mode (iPhone) or Battery Saver (Android).

“Could not create the stream” / sign-in problems

Live streaming must be enabled on the channel: open YouTube Studio → Go live once; YouTube may ask to verify a phone number and then enables streaming within 24 hours. If sign-in keeps failing, sign out from the app’s side menu and sign in again.

I deleted a stream in the app — is it gone from YouTube?

Yes. Deleting a stream removes the broadcast, its recording and its stream key from YouTube. This cannot be undone.

How do I remove the app’s access to my Google account?

Sign out in the side menu, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
