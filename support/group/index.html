A channel for your group

A family, a team or a class can stream into one shared YouTube channel. Everyone sees what is scheduled, who is on air and all recordings in one place. Each member signs in with their own Google account — nobody shares a password.

The shared channel is a YouTube Brand Account. One person — the organizer — creates it once and invites the others. If you are a member who was invited, jump to For members.

How it works

  • The group channel belongs to the organizer's Google account but has its own name and picture.
  • Members are added as Managers of the channel. A manager can stream to it and see its videos; the channel does not get their password, and they do not get the organizer's.
  • In Emdalex Streams everyone signs in with their own Google account and then picks the group channel instead of their own — see Sign in.
  • Each stream belongs to the person who created it: only they can start, edit, finish or delete it. The app marks your own streams with a green dot.

The organizer does steps 1–4 once, on a computer, in a web browser. It takes about ten minutes, plus a one-time wait of up to 24 hours before YouTube allows the new channel to stream.

Step 1 — Create the group channel

  1. Go to youtube.com and sign in with the Google account that will own the group channel (yours, if you are the organizer).
  2. Open youtube.com/channel_switcher. You will see your existing channel (if any) and a tile with a plus sign.
  3. Click Create a channel, enter the group's name (for example Smith Family or Tuesday Swim Group) and click Create. You can leave the picture and the handle as they are.

Step 2 — Enable live streaming

A new channel cannot stream yet. While the group channel is selected on YouTube (your picture, top right → Switch account → the group), follow Enable live streaming: phone verification, YouTube Studio → Create → Go live, then up to 24 hours of waiting. You can do steps 3 and 4 meanwhile.

Step 3 — Check how members are managed

YouTube has two ways to give people access to a channel, and only one of them works with apps like Emdalex Streams: Brand Account managers, added on myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts. People invited through YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions can use the channel on the YouTube website only — not from the app.
  1. Open myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts and click the group channel.
  2. If you see a Manage permissions button under Users, go to Step 4.
  3. If there is no such button, the channel uses the newer permissions system. Switch it back: open studio.youtube.com (with the group channel selected) → Settings (bottom left) → Permissions → Opt out of permissions in YouTube Studio and confirm. Reload the Brand Accounts page — Manage permissions is now there.

Step 4 — Invite the members

  1. On the channel's Brand Account page click Manage permissions. Google may ask for your password again.
  2. Click the invite icon (a person with a plus sign), enter the member's Google e-mail address and choose the role Manager. Add each member this way, then click Invite.
  3. Each member receives an e-mail from Google and must accept it — see below. Until they do, the channel does not appear for them in the app.

Roles. A Manager can stream, watch and delete videos — the right role for members. An Owner can also add and remove people; give it only to a second adult you trust. A Communications manager cannot stream — do not use it.

The organizer streams to the group channel the same way as everyone else: sign in to the app and pick the group channel.

For members: accept and sign in

  1. Open the e-mail from Google titled like “… invited you to manage …” and tap Accept invitation. Use the Google account the e-mail was sent to.
  2. Open Emdalex Streams. If it is already signed in to your own channel, open the side menu (☰) and tap Log Out.
  3. Tap Sign in with Google and choose your own Google account.
  4. On the next screen, Choose your account or a brand account, tap the group channel — not your own name.
  5. Allow access: tick the box next to See, edit, and permanently delete your YouTube videos… and tap Continue — see the screenshot.

The side menu now shows the group's name at the top. You see the same lists as everyone in the group, and you get a notification when someone goes live. To go back to your own channel, log out and sign in choosing your own name.

You do not need a YouTube channel of your own, and you do not need to enable live streaming — the organizer did that for the group channel.

Later: add, remove, change roles

  • Add a member: repeat Step 4.
  • Remove a member: on myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts → the group channel → Manage permissions, click the cross next to their name. The app on their phone loses access to the channel.
  • Change a role: on the same page, choose another role next to the person's name.

Streams stay on the channel when a member leaves. Streams are unlisted by default, so only people with the link — in practice, the group — can watch them.
