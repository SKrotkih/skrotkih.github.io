Never made a video before? Emdalex Studio is for families and beginners. You do not need to know editing: you film short pieces — clips — and the app helps you keep them together as one video, in the right order, with a name and a cover.

  1. Record a clip. Tap the red Create button → New clip and film one part of your story.
  2. Trim it. Drag the yellow handles on the strip of frames to keep only the good part.
  3. Add it to a video. Save the clip into a video — a new one or one you already started.
  4. Put the clips in order. Add more from the camera or your library, reorder them, give the video a name and a cover.
A strip of frames you can touch Edit Clip shows your clip as a row of pictures. Drag the yellow handles to choose where it starts and ends, tap a frame to see it big, press and hold to scrub. Double-tap a frame to zoom into it and cut out a moment in the middle. The original stays as it was until you save.
How it works
Clips — parts of the storyFilm with the app's camera (up to 10 minutes a clip) or add videos you already have in Photos.
Videos — your storiesEach video keeps its clips in order, with a name and a cover photo. The Videos tab lists them all.
Reorder in a dragGrab a clip by its handle and move it where it belongs. The order is saved as soon as you let go.
A cover ready for YouTubePick any photo as the video's cover — the app makes it the right shape and size (1280×720).
Save to PhotosKeep a trimmed clip in your photo library with one tap.
Stays on your phoneYour clips and videos are kept on the iPhone, in the app. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you stream them yourself.
Streams, when you want themThe Streams tab shows your family's live streams and recordings on your own YouTube channel; Go live streams from the camera or a video file. More
No ads, no account with usYou sign in with Google. There are no servers of ours in between and no data collected.

Good to know

Emdalex Studio needs a Google account with a YouTube channel: you sign in with it, and streams go to that channel. Clips and videos do not need the internet — they are made and kept on the phone. Streaming needs live streaming turned on for the channel; see Your own YouTube channel.

How it works with Google

The app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf to show, start, stop and delete live streams on your channel and to list your recordings. Your clips and videos are not sent to Google or to us. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Availability

Emdalex Studio is a paid app for iPhone, coming soon to the App Store; an Android version is on its way. Looking only for live streaming to YouTube? That is Emdalex Streams.
