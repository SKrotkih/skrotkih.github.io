Never made a video before? Emdalex Studio is for families and beginners. You do not need to know editing: you film short pieces — clips — and the app takes you step by step to a finished video, with music, your voice and a cover.

  1. Record or add clips. Film with the app's camera or take videos you already have in Photos.
  2. Trim them. Drag the yellow handles on the strip of frames to keep only the good part, or cut a piece out of the middle.
  3. Build the video. Put the clips in order, give the video a title and a cover, pick a transition.
  4. Add music and your voice. Choose a song, record a voiceover while the video plays.
  5. Export and share. Save the video to Photos, upload it to your own YouTube channel, or stream it live.
From clips to a finished video on one screen Video Builder keeps everything in one scroll: the title and the cover, the clips, the transition, the sound, the export and the finished file. A red button always shows the next step — Export Video, then Save or Stream.
How it works
Clips — parts of the storyFilm with the app's camera (up to 60 minutes a clip) or add videos you already have in Photos.
Trim and cutTrim the start and the end, cut a piece out of the middle — down to a single frame — or replace it with another video. The original clip is never changed.
Always 16:9Ten transitions: dissolve, fade through black or white, slide, push, wipe, zoom, spin, mist. Upright clips sit in the middle over a soft, blurred copy of the picture.
Say the titleTap the microphone and say the title out loud. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it is tidied up right on the phone.
A cover ready for YouTubeMake a 16:9 cover (1280×720): photos, text with outlines, frames, filters, shapes, and Cut Out that keeps just the person.
Music and voiceYour own Sounds library: record your voice, add music from Files, or get free music from Pixabay and the Emdalex Gallery right in the app.
Images for coversKeep pictures for covers in the Images tab — from Photos, Files, the camera, a frame of your clip or the Emdalex Gallery.
Save and shareExport in 720p or 1080p. Save to Photos, upload to your own YouTube channel (unlisted by default), or stream the video live now or later.
On all your iPhonesVideos sync between your iPhones with the same Apple ID through your private iCloud, over Wi-Fi only. Sounds and pictures too, if you choose.
No ads, no account with usYou sign in with Google. There are no servers of ours in between and no data collected.

Good to know

Emdalex Studio needs a Google account with a YouTube channel: you sign in with it, and your uploads and streams go to that channel. Making clips and videos does not need the internet — they are made and kept on the phone. Going live needs live streaming turned on for the channel (YouTube asks for a phone check once; it can take up to 24 hours); see Your own YouTube channel. A scheduled stream starts while Emdalex Studio is open on the iPhone.

Add only music you made or that is free to use: on YouTube, someone else's music can get a video muted or blocked. More in How to add music.

How it works with Google

The app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf: to upload the videos you choose to upload, to start, stop and delete live streams on your channel, and to list your uploads and recordings on My Channel. Your clips and videos are not sent to Google or to us until you upload or stream them yourself. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
