Using Emdalex Studio

Emdalex Studio helps you make a video on your iPhone from short pieces — clips. This page walks you through the app: recording a clip, trimming it, building a video, adding music and your voice, making a cover, and saving or sharing the finished video.

Clips and videos

  • A clip is one piece of your story: something you filmed with the app's camera or added from Photos.
  • A video is the story itself: your clips in order, with a title, a cover and sound. You build it in Video Builder; the Videos tab lists them all.

Clips and videos are kept on your iPhone, inside the app, and in your private iCloud if it is on (more). Nothing goes to YouTube until you upload or stream a video yourself.

Before you start

You sign in with a Google account that has a YouTube channel — any Gmail address is a Google account. No channel yet? Just sign in: the app notices and helps you create one. Step by step: Your own YouTube channel.

When Google asks what Emdalex Studio may use, tick the YouTube box and tap Continue. Making clips and videos works without the internet. Uploading needs the internet; going live also needs live streaming turned on for the channel (how).

Making videos with your family on one shared channel? The organizer sets it up once: A channel for your group.

The main screen

At the bottom there are five places:

  • Videos — every video you are making. Tap one to open it in Video Builder; ⋯ on a row → Edit Video or Delete Video. The + in the header starts a new video. Each row shows the number of clips, the length and how much space the video takes on the iPhone.
  • Sounds — your voice recordings and music, ready to put on a video (more).
  • The round red Create button in the middle opens a panel. Video: New video (name it, then add clips) or New clip (straight to the camera). Streams: Camera or Video file to go live on your channel.
  • My Channel — your uploads, recorded streams and the streams you scheduled (more).
  • Images — pictures for covers (more).

The side menu (☰) has your profile, Settings, Quick Start (the introduction again), this guide, About us and Log Out.

Record a clip

  1. Tap the red Create button → New clip (or, inside a video, the red + → From Camera). The first time, allow the camera and the microphone.
  2. Tap the record button and film. A clip can be up to 60 minutes long. If the iPhone has less free space than that, you see space for N min next to the timer, and recording stops by itself before the storage is full — the clip is kept.
  3. Tap stop. The clip opens in Edit Clip, where you trim it.
  4. Tap Save and choose the video it belongs to — the one you changed last is already chosen, or pick a new video. A clip recorded from inside a video goes straight into that video.

Short clips are easier to put together than one long recording: film one moment at a time.

Edit Clip — trim, cut and replace

Edit Clip shows the clip as a strip of frames. The recorded file itself is never cut: the app only remembers which parts you keep, so you can always bring everything back.

Trim the start and the end
Drag the yellow handles at the two ends of the strip. They move frame by frame; what is left outside them is dimmed. Above the strip you see the length, for example Duration 8.4 s of 15.0 s (what you keep, of the whole clip), under it the current second. Drag a handle to the edge of the screen and the strip scrolls by itself.
Look at a frame
Tap a frame: it gets a white frame and is shown big below the strip. Press and hold, then move your finger, to scrub through the clip. Tap the big picture to keep that moment: Save as Thumbnail makes it the video's cover, Save to Images keeps it in the Images tab.
Cut out a moment in the middle
Double-tap a frame to zoom into it: you see every picture inside it and blue handles. Use them to cut out a bit anywhere; a blue line under the frame shows it was edited.
Cut out a longer part
Tap Cut on the toolbar under Play: a red selection appears on the strip. Move its red handles over the part and tap Delete (or Cancel). A cut part is shown dark with red scissors; tap it → Restore to bring it back.
Replace a part with another video
Select a part with Cut and tap Replace, then pick a video in Photos. The clip becomes the part before the selection, the new video and the part after it — three clips in their place in the video. Nothing is copied: the recording stays one file.
Undo
Undo on the toolbar takes back the last change, one step at a time. ⋯ → Undo all changes brings back the whole clip.
Watch the result
Tap Play: only the part you keep plays, the strip follows along and the red slider shows where you are.
Long clips
A clip longer than 15 minutes opens in pages. The bar above the strip shows the whole clip: tap it to open another page; the yellow handles reach across all pages.
Save and the ⋯ menu
Save on the toolbar keeps your changes. In ⋯: Save to Photos (the trimmed result goes to your photo library), Retake (film the clip again) and Undo all changes. Back and Retake warn you before unsaved changes are lost.

Video Builder

Video Builder is one screen from the first clip to the finished video. From top to bottom:

  1. Title — required (an empty title is outlined in red). It becomes the video's title on YouTube: up to 100 characters, without < and >. Tap the microphone and say the title; on iPhones with Apple Intelligence it is tidied up on the phone. The red check mark saves it.
  2. Thumbnail — the cover (more).
  3. Clips — in the order of the story, each with its picture and length. The red + in the header adds one: From Camera records a new one, From Library takes videos you already have in Photos. Reorder on the right puts them in order (more).
  4. Transition between clips and Frame rate (more).
  5. Sound — music and your voice (more).
  6. Export, Exported video and Video info (more).

Tap a clip to play it. The player goes on to the next clip by itself, and Previous / Next at the top move between them — a quick look at the whole video before you export it (without the transitions and the music). Edit on a row opens Edit Clip. Press and hold a clip → Remove Clip takes it out of the video (the app asks first).

Clips, the cover, the settings and the sound are saved at once.

The video's cover

Tap Add Thumbnail under the title:

  • Choose from Images — a picture from the Images tab becomes the cover. The app makes it the shape and size YouTube expects (16:9, 1280×720).
  • Open Thumbnail Editor — make the cover yourself: Background (a picture or a colour), Photo (pictures on top: move, pinch, turn), Text (font, colour, outline), Frame, Filter, Mask (Cut Out keeps just the person; rounded, circle, star, heart) and Preview. Import Image takes a picture from Images: Use as Thumbnail or Add as Picture.

At the bottom of the editor: Save to Images keeps a copy of the cover in Images, Apply Thumbnail makes it the video's cover. Tap the cover any time to edit it again. To change or remove it, press and hold it → Replace from Images or Remove Thumbnail.

Put clips in order

With two clips or more, tap Reorder in the clips header. Each row gets a Drag handle: drag a clip by it to its place — the order is saved as soon as you let go. Tap Done when you are finished.

Transition and frame rate

Transition between clips shows the one chosen; tap it to choose another — cut, dissolve, fade through black or white, slide, push, wipe, zoom, spin or mist — and its length. Frame rate sets how smooth the exported video is. They are saved at once and used when the video is exported.

The exported video is always 16:9. An upright clip sits in the middle over a soft, blurred copy of its own picture.

Music and voiceover

Under the clips tap Sound → Add Sound. Tap the picture at the top to play the video with its sound as it is now.

Music
Choose Music opens your music from the Sounds tab; tap Use on a song. New songs can be added right there (how). Under the song: Play again until the video ends, Fade in, Fade out, and Replace / Remove.
Your voice
Move the player to where you want to speak and tap Record Voiceover: the video plays while you talk; stop with Stop or at the end of the video. Without headphones the video plays silently, so the speaker does not get into the recording. Add Voice from Sounds takes a recording you made on the Sounds tab. A video can have as many voiceovers as you like.
Volume
Three sliders: Clips' own sound, Music, Voiceover. With Lower the other sound under the voice on, the clips' sound and the music get quieter while you speak.

You hear every change in the player at once. The exported video gets it when it is exported again: tap Export with This Sound.

How to add music to a video

In Video Builder: Sound → Add Sound → Choose Music. You see the music on your Sounds tab, and three ways to add more:

Add from Emdalex Gallery
Free tracks we have picked (public domain), grouped by mood. Tap a track to listen, + to add it to Sounds.
Find Free Music
Opens Pixabay Music inside the app. Pick a song and tap Download: it goes straight into Sounds. Tap Back and use it.
Add from Files — your own music file
The song has to be in the Files app first: AirDrop it from a Mac (it goes to Downloads), put it in iCloud Drive, or in Mail, Messages, Telegram or WhatsApp tap Share → Save to Files. A recording from Voice Memos: Share → Save to Files too.
Which files work
MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV and AIFF. Songs from Apple Music and copy-protected iTunes purchases cannot be added.

The same three buttons are on the Sounds tab → Music, so you can collect songs before you need them.

Rights: use only music you made or that is free to use. On YouTube, someone else's music can get the video muted, blocked or claimed (Content ID). Free music sites have their own license: check it before you use a track. Some Pixabay songs are registered with Content ID — such a claim does not remove the video, the ad money goes to the song's author. On a computer, YouTube's own Audio Library (studio.youtube.com → Audio Library) is a source that Content ID does not claim.

Export, save and share

When the video has clips, the bottom of Video Builder shows its length and size (for example 0:11 · 16:9, 1080p) and a red button with the next step:

  1. Preview plays the whole video full screen, with the transitions and the sound — nothing is made yet.
  2. Export Video makes one video file (16:9, 720p or 1080p) and keeps it on this iPhone. While it is made, its frames appear in the cover at the top. Keep the app open until it is done; Cancel stops it.
  3. Save or Stream opens Exported Video:
    • tap the picture to play it;
    • Save to Photos — a copy in your photo library;
    • Save to YouTube — uploads it to your channel with a title, a description and your cover; unlisted by default. Open on YouTube shows it there;
    • Stream Now — goes live with this video right away;
    • Schedule Stream — goes live with it at the time you choose (Emdalex Studio has to be open on the iPhone then).

Changed something after exporting — a clip, the order, the settings or the sound? Exported video shows a red note that it is out of date: tap Export Again.

Video info shows how much space the video takes on this iPhone. Clean deletes the exported file to free that space; the clips stay, the video can be exported again, and a copy saved to Photos stays in Photos.

The Sounds tab

Sounds is your own library for voiceovers and music, in two parts:

  • Voice — tap Record and speak. Recordings are named Voice 1, Voice 2…
  • Music — Add from Files, Add from Emdalex Gallery or Find Free Music (more).

Tap a row to play it. ⋯ or press and hold: Rename, Trim (keep only part of it), Move to Music / Move to Voice, Delete. A video that already uses a sound keeps its own copy, so deleting it here does not change the video.

The Images tab

Images keeps pictures for covers: Add from Photos, Add from Files, Take Photo or Add from Emdalex Gallery. Frames from Edit Clip and covers from the Thumbnail editor land here too. Tap a picture to see it full screen; press and hold → Rename or Delete. When you choose a cover from a video, the pictures of that video are at the top, under From this video.

iCloud: your videos on all your iPhones

If iCloud is on for the iPhone, your videos — clips, cover and sound — sync between your iPhones signed in to the same Apple ID. They go to your own private iCloud, which only you can see; we have no access to it.

  • Files are sent and received only over Wi-Fi, never over mobile data. A big clip goes in parts and continues where it stopped.
  • The icon on each row on Videos shows whether the video is in iCloud. Settings shows what is still waiting to be sent.
  • The exported video file and scheduled streams stay on the iPhone where they were made.
  • Sounds and Images go to iCloud only one by one, when you choose: tap the cloud icon on a row or under a picture. A crossed-out cloud means it is only on this iPhone.
  • Videos belong to the Google account you are signed in with: another account in the app does not see them.

There is no sync between iPhone and Android.

Streams and My Channel

Emdalex Studio can also go live on your own YouTube channel — for a family event, a match or a lesson. The recording stays on YouTube and takes no space on your phone.

  • Go live: tap the red Create button → Streams → Camera or Video file. A finished video goes live with Stream Now or Schedule Stream on Exported Video. You can also share videos to Emdalex Studio from Photos or Files: they go on air one after another.
  • My Channel → Scheduled — your streams planned for later.
  • My Channel → Upcoming — your stream on air now and those starting soon, with a status line: Live now, Starting soon, Starts in 12 min, or Did not start.
  • My Channel → Recorded — your uploads and finished streams: watch, share or delete them. ⋯ in the header filters them — All, Mine, New (last 7 days) — and opens History, Favorites and Categories.

Streaming works the same as in our streaming app, so its guide applies: going live, pausing, streaming videos from your library, finishing a stream, watching and limits on streaming. Where it says Emdalex Streams, read Emdalex Studio.

Questions and problems

Where are my clips and videos kept?

On your iPhone, inside the app, and — if iCloud is on — in your private iCloud, so your other iPhones with the same Apple ID get them too (more). Deleting a video or a clip in the app deletes it on all of them. A clip or video you saved with Save to Photos stays in Photos.

I added a video from Photos — is it moved out of Photos?

No. The app keeps its own copy as a clip; the video in Photos is not changed.

Do my clips go to YouTube?

No. A video goes to YouTube only when you tap Save to YouTube, Stream Now or Schedule Stream yourself. Uploads are unlisted by default: only people with the link can watch.

A video on my other iPhone has no clips yet

Clips come over Wi-Fi only. Connect the iPhone that made them and the one that waits to Wi-Fi and open the app on both.

Recording stopped by itself

A clip is at most 60 minutes long, and recording also stops before the iPhone's storage is full — the clip is kept either way. Free some space if needed, record the next part as a new clip and add it to the same video.

The export is out of date

You changed a clip, the order, the settings or the sound after exporting. Tap Export Again; the old file is replaced.

Save is greyed out

The video needs a title. Type it in the field at the top.

The app says “This Google account has no YouTube channel yet”

Create a channel for the account, then sign in again: Your own YouTube channel.

The app says “Live streaming is not enabled yet”

YouTube turns on live streaming once per channel, after a phone check and a wait of up to 24 hours: Enable live streaming. Clips, videos and uploads work in the meantime.

The app opened on the sign-in screen by itself

Your Google sign-in has ended — for example, the app's access was removed in your Google account. Sign in again with the same account; your clips and videos are still on the phone.

Remove the app's access to your Google Account

Log Out in the side menu removes the access. If you no longer have the phone, or want to make sure, go to myaccount.google.com/connections (for a group channel — myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts, choose the channel → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services), tap Emdalex Studio → Delete all → Confirm. Your uploads, streams and recordings stay on YouTube; your clips and videos stay on the phone.
