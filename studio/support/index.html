Using Emdalex Studio

Emdalex Studio helps you make a video on your iPhone from short pieces — clips. This page walks you through the app: recording a clip, trimming it, building a video from your clips and, if you want, going live on your own YouTube channel.

Clips and videos

  • A clip is one piece of your story: something you filmed with the app's camera or added from Photos.
  • A video is the story itself: your clips in order, with a name and a cover. You build it on the Videos tab.

Clips and videos are kept on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere while you make them.

Before you start

You sign in with a Google account that has a YouTube channel — any Gmail address is a Google account. No channel yet? Just sign in: the app notices and helps you create one. Step by step: Your own YouTube channel.

When Google asks what Emdalex Studio may use, tick the YouTube box and tap Continue. Clips and videos work without the internet; only streams need it, and they also need live streaming turned on for the channel (how).

Making videos with your family into one shared channel? The organizer sets it up once: A channel for your group.

The main screen

At the bottom there are three places:

  • Videos — every video you are making. Tap one to open it; ⋯ on a row → Edit Video or Delete Video. The + in the header starts a new video.
  • The round red Create button in the middle opens a panel: New video (the same as + on Videos) or New clip (straight to the camera).
  • Streams — live streams and recordings on your YouTube channel (see Streams). A small red dot on it means someone in your group is live right now.

The side menu (☰) has the introduction again, this guide, Settings and Log Out.

Record a clip

  1. Tap the red Create button → New clip. The first time, allow the camera and the microphone.
  2. Tap the record button and film. A clip can be up to 10 minutes long; recording stops by itself at the limit.
  3. Tap stop. The clip opens in Edit Clip, where you trim it.
  4. Tap ⋯ → Save to video. Give the clip a name and choose the video it belongs to — the one you changed last is already chosen, or pick New video.

Short clips are easier to put together than one long recording: film one moment at a time.

Edit Clip — trim and cut

Edit Clip shows the clip as a strip of frames, about one every half second. The recorded file itself is never cut: the app only remembers which parts you keep.

Trim the start and the end
Drag the yellow handles at the two ends of the strip. They move frame by frame; what is left outside them is dimmed. Above the strip you see the length, for example Duration 8.4 s of 15.0 s (what you keep, of the whole clip), under it the current second. Drag a handle to the edge of the screen and the strip scrolls by itself.
Look at a frame
Tap a frame: it gets a white frame and is shown big below the strip. Press and hold, then move your finger, to scrub through the clip.
Cut out a moment in the middle
Double-tap a frame to zoom into it: you see every picture inside it and blue handles. Use them to cut out a bit anywhere; a blue line under the frame shows it was edited.
Cut out a longer part
Tap the scissors left of Play: a red selection appears on the strip. Move its red handles over the part to remove and tap Delete. A cut part is shown dark with red scissors; tap it → Restore to bring it back.
Watch the result
Tap Play at the bottom: only the part you keep plays, the strip follows along and the red slider shows where you are.
The ⋯ menu
Save to video · Save to Photos (the trimmed result goes to your photo library) · Retake (film the clip again) · Undo all changes. Back and Retake warn you before your changes are lost.
Nothing is lost
For a clip that is already in a video, Save keeps your changes at once. Open it in Edit Clip again any time: the handles and cuts are where you left them, and Undo all changes brings back the whole clip.

Build a video

  1. On Videos tap + (or Create → New video). The Video Builder opens.
  2. Type the Title at the top — it is required (an empty title is outlined in red) and becomes the video's title on YouTube: up to 100 characters, without < and >.
  3. Add clips with the red + in the bar at the bottom: From Camera records a new one, From Library takes videos you already have in Photos. An empty video shows the same two buttons in the middle.
  4. Tap Save in the header to keep a changed title. Clips, the cover and the settings are saved at once.

The clips are listed in the order of the story, each with its picture and length. Tap a clip to play it. Edit on its row opens Edit Clip: trim the ends or cut parts out — nothing is lost, the recorded file stays whole, and Undo all changes brings everything back, even after saving. Swipe a row left to remove the clip (the app asks first).

The bar at the bottom: Clips (this list) · + (add a clip) · Assemble (put the clips together, see below).

The video's cover

Tap Thumbnail under the title and pick a photo from your library. The app makes it the shape and size YouTube expects (16:9, 1280×720). Tap the cover to open the editor: background, more photos, text, a frame, filters and masks; Save in the header keeps it. To remove the cover, press and hold the row → Remove Thumbnail.

Put clips in order

With two clips or more, tap Reorder above the list. Each row gets a Drag handle: drag a clip by it to its place — the order is saved as soon as you let go. Tap Done when you are finished.

Video settings

More (the gear) under the title opens Video settings: the transition between clips and its length, and the frame rate. They are saved at once and used when the video is assembled.

Assemble and share the video

  1. Tap Assemble. The player plays the whole video at once, with the transitions — nothing is made yet.
  2. Tap Export Video. The app makes one video file (16:9, 720p or 1080p) and keeps it on this iPhone. Keep the app open until it is done; Cancel stops it.
  3. Tap Save or Stream. On Exported Video:
    • Sound → Add Sound — music or your voice over the video (how);
    • Save to Photos — a copy in your photo library;
    • Save to YouTube — uploads it to your channel;
    • Stream Now — goes live with this video right away;
    • Schedule Stream — goes live with it at the time you choose.

Changed something after exporting — a clip, the order, the settings or the sound? The app shows that the export is out of date: tap Export Again.

Video info on the Assemble screen shows how much space the video takes on this iPhone. Clean deletes the exported file to free that space; the clips stay, the video can be exported again, and a copy saved to Photos stays in Photos.

How to add music to a video

Music and voiceovers are added to a finished video: Videos → a video → Assemble → Save or Stream → Sound → Add Sound. The app takes a music file from the Files app, so the song has to be there first.

Free music
In Add Sound tap Find Free Music — it opens Pixabay Music. Pick a song and tap Download: it is saved to Files → Downloads. Close the browser, tap Add Music from Files → Downloads → the song.
Your own music file
Save it to the Files app first: AirDrop it from a Mac (it goes to Downloads), put it in iCloud Drive, or in Mail, Messages, Telegram or WhatsApp tap Share → Save to Files.
A recording from Voice Memos
Share → Save to Files, then add it with Add Music from Files.
Which files work
MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV and AIFF. Songs from Apple Music and copy-protected iTunes purchases cannot be added.
Rights: use only music you made or that is free to use. On YouTube, someone else's music can get the video muted, blocked or claimed (Content ID). Free music sites have their own license: check it before you use a track. Some Pixabay songs are registered with Content ID — such a claim does not remove the video, the ad money goes to the song's author. On a computer, YouTube's own Audio Library (studio.youtube.com → Audio Library) is a source that Content ID does not claim.

Streams

Emdalex Studio can also go live on your own YouTube channel — for a family event, a match or a lesson. The recording stays on YouTube and takes no space on your phone.

  • Go live: on the Streams tab tap Go live in the header → Camera or Video file. You can also share videos to Emdalex Studio from Photos or Files: they go on air one after another.
  • Upcoming shows streams on air now and those coming up, with a status line: Live now, Starting soon, Starts in 12 min, or Did not start for your own streams that were missed.
  • Recorded lists finished streams. ⋯ in the header filters them — All, Mine, New (last 7 days) — and opens History, Favorites and Categories.

Streaming works the same as in our streaming app, so its guide applies: going live, pausing, streaming videos from your library, finishing a stream, watching and limits on streaming. Where it says Emdalex Streams, read Emdalex Studio.

Questions and problems

Where are my clips and videos kept?

On your iPhone, inside the app. They are part of the app's data, so they go into your iPhone's backup (iCloud or computer) like other apps' data, and they are deleted when you delete them in the app or delete the app. A clip you saved with Save to Photos stays in Photos.

I added a video from Photos — is it moved out of Photos?

No. The app keeps its own copy as a clip; the video in Photos is not changed.

Do my clips go to YouTube?

No. Only a stream goes to YouTube — when you start one yourself.

Recording stopped by itself

A clip is at most 10 minutes long. Record the next part as a new clip and add it to the same video.

Save is greyed out

The video needs a name. Type it in the field at the top.

The app says “This Google account has no YouTube channel yet”

Create a channel for the account, then sign in again: Your own YouTube channel.

The app says “Live streaming is not enabled yet”

YouTube turns on live streaming once per channel, after a phone check and a wait of up to 24 hours: Enable live streaming. Clips and videos work in the meantime.

The app opened on the sign-in screen by itself

Your Google sign-in has ended — for example, the app's access was removed in your Google account. Sign in again; your clips and videos are still on the phone.

Remove the app's access to your Google Account

Log Out in the side menu removes the access. If you no longer have the phone, or want to make sure, go to myaccount.google.com/connections (for a group channel — myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts, choose the channel → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services), tap Emdalex Studio → Delete all → Confirm. Your streams and recordings stay on YouTube; your clips and videos stay on the phone.
