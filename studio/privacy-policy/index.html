Emdalex Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Emdalex Studio (“the app”) is an iPhone app developed by Serhii Krotkih (“we”, “us”). It helps you record clips, build a video from them on your phone — with a cover, music and your voice — and, if you choose, save it to Photos, upload it or stream it live to your own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and where it goes. An Android version is in development; this policy will be updated before it is released.

In short: we have no servers and collect no data. Your videos stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, and go to YouTube only when you upload or stream them to your own channel. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Data the app uses

Your Google / YouTube account

To upload your videos, run live streams and list your uploads and recordings, the app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf. It asks for these Google permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • youtube.force-ssl — upload videos and their covers to your channel, create and manage live broadcasts, list your videos and recordings, change their visibility and delete them when you ask.
  • openid, email, profile — your name, e-mail address and profile picture, shown in the app so you can see which account is signed in.

The Google tokens and the profile information above are stored only on your iPhone (in the iOS Keychain) and are removed when you log out or delete the app. They are sent only to Google’s own servers.

Camera, microphone, Photos and Files

The app records video, photos and audio with the camera and microphone, and reads only the videos, pictures and music files you pick in Photos or Files. Clips, videos, sounds and pictures are kept on your iPhone, inside the app. The app adds a video to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos. Nothing of this is sent to us.

iCloud

If iCloud is on for your iPhone, the app syncs your videos (clips, cover, sound) between your iPhones signed in to the same Apple ID, and the sounds and pictures you choose to put in iCloud. This uses your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only you can read: we cannot see it. Files are transferred only over Wi-Fi. Exported video files and scheduled streams are not synced. You can turn iCloud off for the app in your iPhone’s Settings.

Free music and pictures

  • Pixabay. Find Free Music opens Pixabay Music’s web page inside the app; a song you download there is saved in the app. While you use that page, Pixabay’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Emdalex Gallery. Add from Emdalex Gallery downloads a list of free pictures and music, and the files you pick, from emdalex.com. These are plain files: the app sends nothing about you. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, sees your device’s IP address when the files are requested; we do not receive or keep it.

Speech and Apple Intelligence

When you say a title, or talk about a stream, speech is recognised on the iPhone (Apple on-device speech recognition). On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the words are tidied into a title or notes by Apple’s on-device model. Cut Out in the cover editor finds the person in a picture on the iPhone too. None of this is sent to us.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your current location once, when you create a stream, to suggest a street address for its description. You can edit or delete this text. The address is not stored by the app or used for anything else. The app works without location access.

Contacts and e-mail addresses

If you keep a list of people to tell about your recordings, the addresses you type or pick from Contacts are kept only on your iPhone. The app reads only the contact you pick and has no access to the rest of your contacts. A letter about a recording is written in your mail app, and you send it yourself.

Notifications and alarms

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications and, if you turn it on, an alarm at the start of your own scheduled stream. They are created on the iPhone; no push-notification service of ours is used.

Purchase

The app is bought on the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

2. Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs and does not send data to us. We have no access to your Google account, your iCloud, your videos or your location.

3. Google API Services — Limited Use

Emdalex Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to anyone except as necessary to provide those features.

4. Third parties

Your data is processed only by Google (Google Sign-In, YouTube Data API, YouTube upload, live ingestion and playback), by Apple (iCloud, the App Store) and, when you open them, by Pixabay’s web page and GitHub Pages as described above. See the Google Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Your control

  • Log Out in the app removes the Google tokens and profile data from your iPhone.
  • Revoke the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections (how).
  • Camera, microphone, Photos, speech recognition, location, notifications, alarms and iCloud can be turned off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Clips, videos, sounds and pictures can be deleted in the app; a video deleted on one iPhone is deleted from your iCloud and your other iPhones too. Deleting the app removes what is on that iPhone. Videos on YouTube can be deleted in the app or in YouTube Studio.

6. Families and children

Emdalex Studio is meant to be used by adults, including parents who make videos together with their children. The Google account used in the app must belong to an adult or meet Google’s age requirements. Children may use the app on a parent’s device under the parent’s supervision; the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Videos uploaded to YouTube go to the parent’s own channel, and the parent chooses their visibility (unlisted by default). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.
