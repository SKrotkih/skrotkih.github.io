Emdalex Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Emdalex Studio (“the app”) is an iOS and Android application developed by Serhii Krotkih (“we”, “us”). It helps you plan and shoot short videos with your phone camera, put the clips together into a finished video on your device, and, if you choose, upload it or stream it live to your own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and where it goes.

In short: your videos stay on your phone unless you decide to upload them to your own YouTube channel. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, and contains no advertising or tracking.

1. Data the app uses

Your Google / YouTube account

To upload your videos, run live streams and list your recordings, the app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf. The app requests these Google permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • youtube / youtube.force-ssl / youtube.upload — upload videos to your channel, create and manage live broadcasts, list and delete your videos.
  • openid, email, profile — your name, e-mail address and profile picture, shown in the app so you can see which account is signed in.

The Google access token and the basic profile information above are stored only on your device (iOS Keychain / Android app storage) and are removed when you sign out or delete the app. They are sent only to Google’s own APIs.

Camera and microphone

The app records video and audio with your camera and microphone. Recorded clips and the videos assembled from them are stored on your device, inside the app and, if you choose, in your photo gallery. They are not sent to us. A video leaves your device only when you upload it or go live on your own YouTube channel.

Speech recognition

When you dictate an idea, a title or a description, speech is recognised on the device (Apple on-device speech recognition on iOS, Android on-device speech recognition on Android). Audio is not sent to us.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your current location once, when you create a stream or a video, to suggest a street address for its description. You can edit or delete this text. The address is not stored by the app or used for anything else. The app works without location access.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device, for example to remind you of a scheduled stream. They are created on the device; no push-notification service is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the store only tells the app whether a subscription is active.

2. Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not send data to us or to third parties other than Google/YouTube and the app store as described above. We have no access to your Google account, your videos or your location.

3. Google API Services — Limited Use

Emdalex Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to anyone except as necessary to provide those features.

4. Third parties

Your data is processed only by Google (Google Sign-In, YouTube Data API, YouTube upload, live ingestion and playback) and by the app store that handles your subscription (Apple or Google). See the Google Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Your control

  • Sign out in the app to remove the Google token and profile data from your device.
  • Revoke the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Camera, microphone, speech recognition, location and notification permissions can be changed at any time in your phone’s Settings.
  • Clips and projects on your device can be deleted in the app; deleting the app removes them. Videos on YouTube can be deleted in the app or in YouTube Studio.

6. Families and children

Emdalex Studio is meant to be used by adults, including parents who shoot videos together with their children. The Google account used in the app must belong to an adult or meet Google’s age requirements. Children may use the app on a parent’s device under the parent’s supervision; the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Videos uploaded to YouTube go to the parent’s own channel, and the parent chooses their visibility. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.
